# Support

- For reproducible bugs, open a [bug report](https://github.com/ahmedjawedaj/quantaora/issues/new?template=bug.yml).
- For new labs or learning improvements, open a [lab proposal](https://github.com/ahmedjawedaj/quantaora/issues/new?template=lab-proposal.yml).
- For security or privacy concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

Please include the affected route, device/browser, expected result, actual result, and screenshots where useful. Quantaora is a community alpha; support is best-effort and no response-time guarantee is provided.
