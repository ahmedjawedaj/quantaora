# Rotation & Revolution

*Space > Earth In Space > Earth's Motion & Cycles > Earth · two motions at once > Rotation & Revolution*

## 1. Two motions, two very different lengths

Rotation is Earth spinning in place like a top — one full turn takes about 24 hours, which is exactly what defines a day. Revolution is Earth travelling all the way around the Sun — one full lap takes about 365.25 days, which is exactly what defines a year.

## 2. Speed one up without touching the other

Drag the rotation slider and only the spin changes — Earth still travels around the Sun at the same pace. Drag the revolution slider and only the orbit speed changes — Earth still spins on its axis at the same rate. In real life the ratio between them is fixed (about 365 spins per lap); here you can break that ratio on purpose to see each motion clearly on its own.

## 3. Watch the sunlit side

As Earth spins, the model lights up on the side facing the Sun and stays dark on the far side — that's rotation causing day and night in real time, exactly the way real sunlight only ever lights up half of Earth at once.

## 4. The everyday mix-up

"Earth rotates around the Sun" is one of the most common science mix-ups — rotation is the spin, revolution is the orbit. Only revolution actually goes around the Sun.
