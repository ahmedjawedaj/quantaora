# Supernova

*Space > Stars > Stellar Lifecycle > Violent death > Supernova*

A star's whole life, ending in one wave — the factory of every heavy element, including of you.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a layered ball like an onion — the huge star — then press Play. Watch the core suddenly collapse, the shock rebound, and the shell blast outward into a spreading, glowing cloud dozens of times larger than the star.

## 2. Touch it

Drag the timeline slider to repeat the explosion slowly. At one point the model pauses with the core still frozen: toggle `hs-iron-core` to show the iron fuse, then continue to watch the bounce and the outrush. Tap the element counter to see gold, silver, and uranium tick up as the blast cools.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-iron-core` for the fatal fuse, `hs-collapsing-envelope` for the infalling layers, `hs-shockwave` for the rebound that launched everything, and `hs-remnant-shell` for the expanding debris shell left behind.

## 4. Explain it

Walk the chain in under a minute: as the core fuses its way to iron, fusion stops producing energy → gravity collapses the whole core in ~1 second → the collapse overshoots and rebounds → the shock blasts through the envelope → heat and speed forge heavy elements → the shell keeps expanding for thousands of years while the core becomes a neutron star or black hole, and the elements are scattered.

## 5. Predict it

Before you drag the slider to the rebound, ask yourself: *when a ball bounces, which way does it push?* Watch the shock bounce off the collapsed core and fly outward.

**Prediction prompt:** The blast outshines the whole galaxy for weeks. Predict how close a supernova would have to be for it to be dangerous to humans — then check the distance readout on the model (about 50 light-years).

## 6. Belongs to

Supernova is the violent-death chapter of the Stars branch: it births the neutron star and sometimes the black hole, and it is the other end of the spectrum from the white dwarf's gentle nebula.

## 7. Compare it

Run the gentle planetary-nebula scene from the White Dwarf component side by side: two deaths of stars. Which one occurred in a low-mass star, which in a massive star? Which one leaves a 20 km remnant? Which one builds gold?

## 8. Review it later

**Practice item:** Describe the supernova in four points without looking: fuse, collapse, bounce, scatter.

**Checkpoint:** Can you explain why your body's iron and gold came from stars, and why the explosion can outshine a whole galaxy?
