# Reactant

*Chemistry > Chemical Reactions > Reading Reactions > The starting materials > Reactant*

A reactant is a substance that starts a chemical reaction — the starting material that gets consumed and transformed. It is written on the left side of a chemical equation.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a small chemical equation floating in space — on the left, molecules of hydrogen and oxygen; an arrow in the middle; on the right, a water molecule. Tap the `hs-reactant-materials` hotspot to open its card. The molecules on the left are the reactants.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate the equation and zoom into the left side. Toggle the 'combine' layer to watch the hydrogen and oxygen molecules approach, react, and rearrange. Toggle the 'arrow' layer to emphasize the arrow — 'reacts to make' — that separates reactants from products.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-reactant-materials` to label the reactants, `hs-reactant-combine` to label them reacting, and `hs-reactant-consumed` to label them being used up as water forms.

## 4. Explain what it does

Walk through what a reactant does: it is the starting material of a reaction, written on the left of the arrow. As the reaction proceeds, reactants are consumed — but not destroyed. Their atoms are rearranged and end up in the products on the right.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you toggle the combine layer on, predict what happens to the hydrogen and oxygen atoms — do they disappear, or do they reappear in the water?

## 5. See where it belongs

A reactant belongs in a **chemical equation**, on the left of the arrow. From there it connects to the **product** it becomes, the **coefficient** that counts how many of it react, and the **balanced equation** that tracks its atoms.

## 6. Compare it

Compare a **reactant** with a **product**. A reactant is the starting material — consumed on the left of the arrow, like ingredients. A product is the new substance — created on the right, like the cooked dish. Every reaction turns reactants into products.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to answer a real question: *In hydrogen + oxygen → water, which substances are the reactants and where do they sit in the equation?* Point to the left side and name them.

**Practice item:** On the model, point to a reactant molecule and explain aloud what happens to its atoms when the reaction runs.
