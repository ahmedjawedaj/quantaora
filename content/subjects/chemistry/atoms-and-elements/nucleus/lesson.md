# Nucleus

*Chemistry > Atoms And Elements > Inside The Atom > The atom's core > Nucleus*

The nucleus is the dense, positively charged core of an atom, packed with protons and neutrons. It is tiny — but it holds nearly all of the atom's mass.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a tiny dense bundle of red and gray spheres at the center, surrounded by a huge translucent cloud with a wide empty gap between them. Tap the `hs-nucleus-core` hotspot to open its card. The bundle is the nucleus.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate the model to appreciate the size contrast — the tiny nucleus versus the huge cloud. Toggle the 'scale' layer to shrink the cloud away and reveal how small and dense the core really is. Toggle the 'pull' layer to see the nucleus's positive charge tugging the electron cloud inward.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-nucleus-core` to label the nucleus, `hs-nucleus-particles` to label the protons and neutrons inside, and `hs-nucleus-pull` to label the positive charge that holds the atom together.

## 4. Explain what it does

Walk through what the nucleus does: it is the dense positive core holding the protons and neutrons, it carries nearly all the atom's mass, and its positive charge pulls the negative electrons into orbit across the vast empty gap.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you toggle the scale layer on, predict how big the nucleus will look compared to the electron cloud — then see the real contrast.

## 5. See where it belongs

The nucleus belongs at the center of the **atom**. From there it connects to the **protons** and **neutrons** packed inside, and across to the **electron shells** that wrap far around it.

## 6. Compare it

Compare the **nucleus** with the **electron shells**. The nucleus is tiny, dense, and holds the mass; the shells are huge, nearly empty, and hold the electrons that do chemistry. One is the engine, the other is the workshop.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to answer a real question: *An atom is mostly empty space. Where, exactly, is its mass?* Point to the nucleus and explain what is packed inside it.

**Practice item:** On the model, point to the nucleus and explain aloud why it holds nearly all the atom's mass despite being so small.

## 8. Review it later

**Checkpoint:** What is the nucleus, what is inside it, and why does it hold nearly all the atom's mass?
