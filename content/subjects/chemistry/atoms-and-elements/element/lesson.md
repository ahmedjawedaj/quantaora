# Element

*Chemistry > Atoms And Elements > The Atom > The smallest piece of matter > Element*

An element is a pure substance made of only one kind of atom. There are about 90 natural elements, and everything is built from them.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: several floating samples of pure elements — a chunk of gold, a block of carbon, a cloud of oxygen, a puff of helium. Tap the `hs-element-samples` hotspot to open its card. Each one is made of a single kind of atom.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate the samples and zoom in. Toggle the 'zoom in' layer to dive into the gold chunk and see its atoms — every single one a gold atom. Toggle the 'combine' layer to watch a hydrogen atom and an oxygen atom bond into water — a new substance from two elements.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-element-samples` to label the element samples, `hs-element-atoms` to label the identical atoms inside, and `hs-element-combine` to label the elements bonding into a compound.

## 4. Explain what it does

Walk through what an element is: a pure substance of only one kind of atom, defined by its proton count. Gold is all gold atoms, carbon is all carbon atoms. Elements cannot be broken down chemically into anything simpler, but they combine into compounds and molecules.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you toggle the zoom-in layer on, predict whether every atom in the gold chunk will be the same kind or different kinds.

## 5. See where it belongs

An element belongs to the family of **pure substances**. From there it connects down to the **atoms** that make it up and the **protons** that define it, and across to **compounds** — what elements build when they bond.

## 6. Compare it

Compare an **element** with a **compound**. An element is a pure substance of ONE kind of atom — gold is all gold. A compound is a pure substance of DIFFERENT elements bonded — water is hydrogen + oxygen. Elements are raw ingredients; compounds are cooked dishes.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to answer a real question: *Why can you not break an element like gold into anything simpler by chemical means?* Point to the atoms and explain what an element is.

**Practice item:** On the combine layer, point to the hydrogen and oxygen atoms becoming water and explain aloud how two elements make a new substance.
