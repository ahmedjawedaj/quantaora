# Muscles

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Muscular system > Muscles*

Muscles are tissues that can shorten (contract) to pull on bones and create movement. There are three types. Skeletal muscle attaches to bones and moves your body when you decide to move - it is striated (striped) and under your control. Smooth muscle is found in organs like the stomach and blood vessels; it works automatically. Cardiac muscle is only in the heart, beats automatically, and never tires. Skeletal muscles work in pairs: when one shortens (contracts) another relaxes, and together they move a joint. Muscles need energy and use it when they work, which is why you get tired and breathe faster during exercise.
