# Brain

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Nervous system > Brain*

The brain is the body's control center, made of billions of neurons (nerve cells) packed together. It has several main regions. The cerebrum, the big wrinkled outer part, handles thinking, sensing, and voluntary movement; its two halves are the right and left hemispheres, and its lobes do different jobs. The cerebellum at the back coordinates balance and smooth movement. The brainstem connects the brain to the spinal cord and runs automatic life jobs like breathing and heartbeat. Deep inside, the hippocampus helps form memories and the amygdala helps with emotions. The brain floats in fluid that cushions it, and is protected by the skull.
