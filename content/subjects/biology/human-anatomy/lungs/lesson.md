# Lungs

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Respiratory system > Lungs*

The lungs are two soft, spongy organs inside the chest. When you breathe in, air travels down your windpipe and divides into branching tubes (bronchi and bronchioles) that end in millions of tiny clusters of air sacs called alveoli. The alveoli are wrapped in a network of tiny blood vessels, and it is here that oxygen crosses into the blood and carbon dioxide crosses out to be breathed away. The lungs are covered by a smooth membrane and are protected by the ribcage. The right lung has three lobes and the left lung has two, because the heart sits on the left side.

## 4. Explain it

Air travels through a branching tree of tubes to reach the alveoli. There, oxygen crosses into the blood and carbon dioxide crosses out to be breathed away - this is gas exchange.
