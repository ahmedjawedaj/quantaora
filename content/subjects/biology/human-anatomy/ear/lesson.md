# Ear

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Sensory systems > Ear*

The ear has three main parts. The outer ear (the visible part plus the ear canal) funnels sound waves toward the eardrum. The middle ear has three tiny bones (hammer, anvil, stirrup) that pass the vibrations on and make them stronger. The inner ear contains the coiled cochlea, which turns the vibrations into nerve signals, and the semicircular canals, which help with balance. The signals from the cochlea travel along the auditory nerve to the brain, which turns them into the sounds you recognize. So hearing is a chain: sound waves, vibrations, signals, brain.
