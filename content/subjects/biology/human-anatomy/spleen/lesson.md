# Spleen

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Immune / lymphatic system > Spleen*

The spleen is a small organ about the size of a fist, tucked under the ribs on the left side. It acts like a blood filter and immune checkpoint. It filters the blood, recycling old or damaged red blood cells and salvaging useful parts like iron. It also stores blood cells and platelets for when the body needs them. And it helps the immune system: white blood cells in the spleen can spot and respond to germs in the blood. If the spleen is removed, other organs can take over most of its jobs, though the person needs extra protection from some infections.
