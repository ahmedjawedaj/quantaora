# Pancreas

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Digestive system / Endocrine system > Pancreas*

The pancreas is a soft, elongated gland tucked behind the stomach. It has two very different jobs. For digestion, it makes pancreatic juice - a liquid full of enzymes - and sends it to the small intestine to help break down proteins, fats, and carbs. For balance, it makes hormones (especially insulin) and releases them into the blood. Insulin signals cells to take in sugar from the blood, keeping blood-sugar levels steady. So the pancreas is both a digestion helper and a blood-sugar controller.
