# Stomach

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Digestive system > Stomach*

The stomach is a J-shaped muscular bag at the top of the abdomen. When food arrives from the esophagus, the stomach stretches and churns it with strong muscles, mixing it with gastric juices - acid and enzymes that start breaking down the food, especially proteins. The acid also kills many germs that come in with food. After a few hours, the food has become a soupy mixture called chyme, which moves into the small intestine. The stomach has a thick lining with special cells that make the acid and enzymes, and a layer of mucus protects the stomach wall from digesting itself.
