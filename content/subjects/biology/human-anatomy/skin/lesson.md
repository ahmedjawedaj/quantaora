# Skin

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Integumentary system > Skin*

The skin is the body's largest organ - in an adult it covers about 2 square meters. It has two main layers. The outer epidermis is the thin protective surface; its outer cells wear away and are constantly replaced. Below it, the thicker dermis contains blood vessels, nerves that sense touch, sweat glands, oil glands, and hair roots. The skin has many jobs: it protects the body from damage, germs, and water loss; it helps control temperature through sweating and blood flow; it lets you feel touch, pressure, heat, and cold; and it repairs itself when cut. A deeper layer, the hypodermis, attaches it to the body.
