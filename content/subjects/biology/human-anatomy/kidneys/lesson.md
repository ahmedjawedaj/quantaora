# Kidneys

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Urinary system > Kidneys*

The kidneys are two bean-shaped organs at the back of the abdomen, one on each side of the spine. Their job is to filter the blood. Every few minutes all of your blood passes through the kidneys, where tiny filtering units called nephrons take out waste products and extra water. That waste becomes urine, which travels through tubes to the bladder to be stored and later released. The kidneys also help keep the right balance of water and salts in your body and make hormones that help control blood pressure and make red blood cells. When your kidneys filter, the cleaned blood returns to your circulation.
