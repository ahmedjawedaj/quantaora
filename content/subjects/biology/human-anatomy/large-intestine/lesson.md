# Large Intestine

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Digestive system > Large Intestine*

The large intestine is the last major part of the digestive system. It is wider than the small intestine but much shorter (about 1.5 meters). By the time food reaches it, most nutrients have been absorbed. The large intestine's main job is to absorb water and salts from the leftover mixture, making the waste more solid. Helpful bacteria live here too, helping process some materials and making some vitamins. The solid waste is stored in the last part (the rectum) until it leaves the body. So the large intestine is where the body salvages the last water and packs up what it cannot use.
