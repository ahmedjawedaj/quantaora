# Eye

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Sensory systems > Eye*

The eye works like a camera. Light enters through the clear front (cornea) and passes through a hole called the pupil, whose size is controlled by the colored iris. The lens behind the pupil focuses the light onto the back of the eye, a screen called the retina. The retina is covered with light-sensing cells that turn light into signals. Those signals travel through the optic nerve to the brain, which builds the picture you see. The eyeball sits in a protective socket, and the sclera is its tough white outer coat. This whole path - light in, focused, sensed, sent - is how you see.
