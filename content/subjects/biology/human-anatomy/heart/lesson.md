# Heart

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Cardiovascular system > Heart*

The heart is a muscular pump about the size of your fist, found in the chest just behind the breastbone. It has four chambers: two smaller ones on top (atria) that receive blood, and two bigger ones below (ventricles) that pump blood out. Valves open and close to keep blood moving in one direction. The heart actually works as a double pump: the right side sends blood to the lungs to pick up oxygen, and the left side pumps the oxygen-rich blood to the whole body. It beats about 60-100 times a minute, day and night, without ever stopping.

## 4. Explain it

The heart is a double pump. The right side sends oxygen-poor blood to the lungs, the left side sends oxygen-rich blood to the whole body. Valves keep the flow one-way, and cardiac muscle beats constantly.
