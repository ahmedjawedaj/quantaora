# Liver

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Digestive system > Liver*

The liver is the largest internal organ, sitting under the ribs on the right side. It is a chemical factory with over 500 jobs. It processes the nutrients that come from digested food, makes bile (a liquid that helps break down fats in digestion), stores sugar as a reserve, removes harmful substances (like alcohol and medicines) from the blood, and makes important proteins. Because blood from the digestive system flows through the liver first, the liver gets to process everything you eat. It is made of special cells arranged in neat plates, with blood flowing between them.
