# Bones

*Biology > Human Anatomy > Skeletal system > Bones*

Bones are the hard parts of the body that form the skeleton. They are made of a mix of hard minerals (like calcium) and flexible protein (collagen), which together make them strong yet slightly bendy. Bones have several jobs: they support the body and give it shape, they protect soft organs (like the brain and heart), they work with muscles to let you move (the muscles pull on bones at joints), and inside many bones is bone marrow that makes blood cells. Bones are alive - they grow, repair themselves when broken, and store calcium. There are about 206 bones in an adult skeleton.
