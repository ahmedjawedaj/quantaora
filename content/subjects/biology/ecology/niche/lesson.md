# Niche

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Niche*

A niche is the role a species plays in its ecosystem - its 'job.' It includes what the species eats, what eats it, when it is active (day or night), and how it uses the resources around it. For example, a honeybee's niche includes pollinating flowers, collecting nectar, and being food for some birds. Two different species cannot hold exactly the same niche in the same place for long - they compete, and one usually wins. A niche is like a species' career; the habitat is just its address.
