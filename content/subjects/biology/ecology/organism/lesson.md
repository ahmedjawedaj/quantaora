# Organism

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Organism*

An organism is any living thing: an oak tree, a dog, a mushroom, a bacterium. Every organism is made of cells and can carry out life activities - it grows, uses energy, responds to its surroundings, makes copies of itself (reproduces), and keeps its inside balanced. Organisms come in every size, from a single microscopic cell to a blue whale. Each organism is one living individual, and together individuals form populations, communities, and ecosystems.
