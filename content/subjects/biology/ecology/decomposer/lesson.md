# Decomposer

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Decomposer*

A decomposer breaks down dead things - fallen leaves, dead animals, and waste - and returns their materials to the soil. Fungi, bacteria, and earthworms are the main decomposers. As they eat and break down dead matter, they release nutrients like nitrogen and minerals back into the soil, where plants can use them again. Decomposers are the ecosystem's recyclers: without them, nutrients would stay locked up in dead bodies and the soil would slowly run out of the materials life needs.
