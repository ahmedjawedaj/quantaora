# Habitat

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Habitat*

A habitat is the natural home of a species - the place where it lives, finds food and water, gets shelter, and raises young. A fish's habitat might be a pond; a camel's habitat is the desert; a worm's habitat is the soil. A good habitat provides everything the organism needs: food, water, shelter, and the right space. If a habitat changes (like a forest being cut down), the organisms that live there can lose their home and may not survive. Each species has a preferred habitat, and protecting habitats is key to protecting species.

## 4. Explain it

A habitat is a species' natural home. It must provide food, water, shelter, and a place to raise young. If the habitat changes or disappears, the species is threatened.
