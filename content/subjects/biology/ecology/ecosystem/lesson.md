# Ecosystem

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Ecosystem*

An ecosystem is everything in an area working together: all the living things (the community) plus the nonliving things around them, like water, soil, sunlight, air, and temperature. In an ecosystem, the living things depend on the nonliving parts - plants need sunlight and water, animals need plants, and decomposers recycle dead material into soil. Energy flows through the ecosystem (from sun to plants to animals), and matter is recycled. An ecosystem can be as small as a pond or a rotting log, or as large as a whole desert or forest.
