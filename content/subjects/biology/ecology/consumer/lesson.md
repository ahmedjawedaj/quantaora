# Consumer

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Consumer*

A consumer cannot make its own food, so it eats other living things to get energy. There are three main kinds. Herbivores eat only plants (like a rabbit). Carnivores eat other animals (like a lion). Omnivores eat both plants and animals (like a bear or a person). All consumers depend, directly or indirectly, on producers - the plants - because that is where the food energy originally comes from. Consumers sit above producers in the food chain.

## 4. Explain it

A consumer cannot make its own food, so it eats other living things. Herbivores eat plants, carnivores eat animals, and omnivores eat both - and all of them depend on producers.
