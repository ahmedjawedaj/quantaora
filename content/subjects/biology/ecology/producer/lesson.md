# Producer

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Producer*

A producer makes its own food. The most common producers are green plants, which use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make their own food (sugar) through photosynthesis. Algae, phytoplankton, and some bacteria are also producers. Because producers create food from sunlight and simple ingredients, they are the first link in every food chain - everything else ultimately depends on them for energy. Without producers, there would be no food for anything else.
