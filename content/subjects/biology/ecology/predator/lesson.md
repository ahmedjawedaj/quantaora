# Predator

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Predator*

A predator is an animal that hunts and eats other animals. Predators come in many forms - a lion, an eagle, a wolf, or a dragonfly all hunt for their food. To be good hunters, predators are often equipped with speed, sharp senses, sharp teeth or claws, and good camouflage. Predators matter to an ecosystem because they keep prey populations in check - without predators, prey numbers can grow too high and damage their own food supply. Predators and prey exist in a constant race of better hunting against better escaping.
