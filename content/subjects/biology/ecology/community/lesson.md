# Community

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Community*

A community is all the living things of all different species in one area, interacting together. A forest community includes every tree, bird, insect, mushroom, and microbe in the forest. Members of a community interact in many ways: some eat others, some compete for food or space, some help each other (like flowers and pollinators), and some live on or inside others. The community does not include the nonliving parts of the area - those belong to the ecosystem, which is the community plus its surroundings.

## 4. Explain it

A community is all the living things of all species in an area. They interact by eating, competing, and helping each other - but rocks and water are not part of the community; they belong to the ecosystem.
