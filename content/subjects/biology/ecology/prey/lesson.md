# Prey

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Prey*

Prey is any animal that predators hunt and eat. Rabbits, mice, deer, and fish are common prey. To survive, prey have their own adaptations: many are fast, some blend into their surroundings with camouflage, some hide in burrows, and many live in groups so there are more eyes watching for danger. Prey and predators are locked in a constant race - as predators get faster, prey get faster too, and this balance keeps both populations healthy. A healthy ecosystem needs both prey and predators.
