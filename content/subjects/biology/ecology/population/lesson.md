# Population

*Biology > Ecology > Ecology Foundations > Ecological levels and roles > Population*

A population is a group of organisms of the same species that live in the same place at the same time. All the dandelions in one field, all the squirrels in one forest - each is a population. Populations grow when births and new arrivals outnumber deaths and departures, and they shrink when the reverse happens. Population size can be limited by food, water, space, disease, and predators. Studying populations helps us understand how species are doing and what an area can support.

## 4. Explain it

A population is all the members of one species in one place. It grows when births outnumber deaths and shrinks when deaths outnumber births, and its size is limited by food, water, space, and disease.
