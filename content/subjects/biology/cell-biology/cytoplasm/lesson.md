# Cytoplasm

*Biology > Cell Biology > Cell Structures > Cell structures (organelles) > Cytoplasm*

The cytoplasm is the jelly-like material that fills the cell. It holds the organelles, lets materials move around, and hosts many chemical reactions. It is the living 'workspace' of the cell.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a translucent rounded space filled with a soft, fluid-looking material. Inside it, notice the nucleus and other organelles floating. The cytoplasm is the filling that surrounds them all, held inside the cell membrane.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate the model and zoom in. Toggle the 'streaming' layer to watch small dots drift slowly through the fluid, carrying a material from one side toward the other. Pause it and look at how each organelle is surrounded by the same filling.

## 3. Name it

Tap the `hs-cytosol` hotspot to label the fluid. Tap `hs-organelles` to label where the organelles float in the cytoplasm. Tap `hs-transport` to label the streaming movement that carries materials, and `hs-fill` to label the whole filling inside the membrane.

## 4. Explain it

Walk through what the cytoplasm does. It supports the organelles, it is the route by which materials are delivered between them, and it is where many reactions happen. Because it is mostly water, it can flow, letting the cell move materials around and change shape.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you watch the streaming layer, predict which direction a small material dot will travel when moving from the nucleus toward the cell membrane — then check.

## 5. Belongs to

The cytoplasm belongs to the **eukaryotic cell** as its interior workspace. It is everything inside the cell membrane except the nucleus. From there it connects to bigger ideas: materials move across the cell membrane into the cytoplasm, and reactions in the cytoplasm feed into the work of every organelle.

## 6. Compare it

Compare the cytoplasm with the **cytosol**. The cytosol is only the fluid part — water with dissolved molecules. The cytoplasm is the whole filling: the cytosol plus all the organelles floating in it. So every cell has cytosol, and the cytoplasm is the full workspace that includes it.
