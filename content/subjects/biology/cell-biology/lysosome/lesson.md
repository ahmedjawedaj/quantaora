# Lysosome

*Biology > Cell Biology > Cell Structures > Cell structures (organelles) > Lysosome*

The lysosome is a small sac full of powerful enzymes that digest things. Its job is to break down waste, old or damaged cell parts, and materials taken in from outside - even germs. It can also recycle the pieces, so the cell can use them again. The enzymes inside work best in the lysosome's special acidic environment, and the sac's membrane keeps these powerful digesters safely contained so they do not harm the rest of the cell. Think of the lysosome as the cell's recycling and cleanup crew.
