# Chloroplast

*Biology > Cell Biology > Cell Structures > Cell structures (organelles) > Chloroplast*

The chloroplast is the organelle where plants make their own food. It is green because it contains a pigment called chlorophyll that captures energy from sunlight. Inside, chloroplasts have stacks of flat discs called thylakoids (grana), and the space around them is the stroma. Using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide, the chloroplast performs photosynthesis: it makes sugar (glucose) and releases oxygen. That sugar is food for the plant (and ultimately for almost all life).

## 4. Explain it

Chlorophyll in the stacks captures sunlight. The chloroplast uses that energy, plus water and carbon dioxide, to make sugar and release oxygen - this is photosynthesis, how plants make their own food.
