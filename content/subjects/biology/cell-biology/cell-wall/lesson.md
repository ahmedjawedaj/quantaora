# Cell Wall

*Biology > Cell Biology > Cell Structures > Cell structures (organelles) > Cell Wall*

The cell wall is a thick, rigid layer that wraps around the outside of plant cells, just beyond the cell membrane. It is mostly made of a tough carbohydrate called cellulose. Its job is to support and protect the cell and give it its fixed shape, like a strong brick wall around a room. The wall is porous, so water and dissolved materials can still pass through, and the membrane inside keeps controlling what enters the cell. Plants, fungi, and many bacteria have cell walls; animal cells do not.
