# Protozoa

*Biology > Microbiology > Microbes > Microbial types > Protozoa*

Protozoa are tiny organisms made of a single cell, larger than bacteria and with a true nucleus. Many can move and hunt like tiny animals, and they live in ponds, oceans, soil, and inside other living things.

## 1. See it

## 4. Explain what it does

A protozoan is a complete living animal in just one cell. It senses its surroundings, moves using a flagellum (one whip tail), cilia (many tiny hairs), or pseudopods (false feet the amoeba stretches out), and it eats by pulling food into a little sac called a food vacuole. Because it has a true nucleus, its single cell works like a full living cell - a whole tiny organism in one cell.

## 5. See where it belongs

Protozoa belong to the microbial world with bacteria, fungi, and viruses. Unlike bacteria and viruses, they are single-celled but with a true nucleus. They live in ponds, oceans, and soil, and some live in the bodies of animals and people, where a few can cause illness.

## 6. Compare it

Compare a protozoan to a **bacterium**. Both are single-celled, but a protozoan is larger, has a true nucleus, and often actively moves and hunts. A bacterium is smaller, has no true nucleus, and is usually more passive. For this reason protozoa behave more like tiny animals.
