# Leaf

*Biology > Plants > Plant Structure > Plant organs > Leaf*

A leaf is the plant's food factory. It is green because of chlorophyll - a pigment that captures sunlight. Water and carbon dioxide go in, and with the help of the sun the leaf makes sugar (food) and releases oxygen.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a single green leaf on a short stalk, thin and broad. A cutaway slice near the middle reveals the inner sponge-like tissue and the veins. Notice the edges of the leaf and the tiny pores on its surface.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate and zoom in on the leaf. Toggle the layers to make the leaf transparent and see the network of veins running through it. Then look closely at the surface to find the tiny pores called stomata.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-leaf-blade` to label the broad green blade, `hs-vein` to label the veins carrying water and food, `hs-chlorophyll` to label the green pigment, and `hs-stomata` to label one of the tiny pores.

## 4. Explain what it does

Follow the steps of photosynthesis: sunlight strikes the leaf → chlorophyll captures the energy → the leaf takes in water (from the roots) and carbon dioxide from the air through the stomata → it uses the sunlight energy to join these together into sugar (food) → it releases oxygen as a byproduct → the sugar is carried out to the rest of the plant through the veins.

**Prediction prompt:** Predict what you think would happen if you covered a leaf with a dark bag so no light reaches it for several days. Which part of the photosynthesis equation is missing?

**Practice item:** Put the photosynthesis ingredients in order using the model: what does the leaf take in, and what does it make? Name the two gases/image inputs the leaf uses.

## 5. See where it belongs

The leaf is one organ of the whole plant. Water arrives via the stem from the root (carried in xylem), and the food it makes travels back out through phloem to feed the stem, roots, flowers, and stored reserves. The leaf is the plant's solar-powered kitchen.

## 6. Compare it

Compare the leaf to the **root**. The root is a compact underground organ that absorbs water and minerals from the soil. The leaf is a broad, flat, green organ that catches sunlight and makes food. One takes in water from below; the other captures energy from above.

## 7. Use it in a task

A plant is placed in a dark closet. Predict what the plant will still be able to do and what it will gradually be unable to do, and connect it to the leaf's job. Then say what a plant would most need if you wanted to see healthy, broad leaves.

**Checkpoint:** Can you name the three ingredients a leaf uses and the two things it makes during photosynthesis?
