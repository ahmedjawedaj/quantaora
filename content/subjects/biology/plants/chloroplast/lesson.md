# Chloroplast

*Biology > Plants > Plant Function > Organelles inside plant cells > Chloroplast*

A chloroplast is the green food-making factory inside a plant cell. It captures sunlight and uses it, with water and carbon dioxide, to make the plant's food and release oxygen.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: an oval green organelle floating, cut open to show its inside. The inside holds stacks of flat green discs (thylakoids). Around them is a fluid space. Notice the outer membrane wrapping the whole thing as dots of light strike it.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate and zoom in. Toggle the outer membrane off to see inside. Toggle the disc stacks to highlight them. Turn on the 'sunlight' controller to watch light dots strike the chloroplast and small oxygen dots float away while a sugar-sweet cascade of food gathers.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-outer` to label the outer membrane, `hs-grana` to label a stack of thylakoid discs, `hs-stroma` to label the fluid space, and `hs-chlorophyll` to label the green pigment that captures light. Tap `hs-output` to mark the food and oxygen released.

## 4. Explain what it does

Follow the steps: sunlight hits the chlorophyll → the pigment captures the light energy → in the discs, this energizes a reaction → the chloroplast combines that captured energy with water and carbon dioxide (which enters through stomata) → making sugar (the plant food) and releasing oxygen → the sugar feeds the plant, the oxygen leaves to the air. This little organelle is the whole photosynthesis engine of a leaf cell.

**Prediction prompt:** In a dark room a leaf cell still holds its chloroplasts. Predict what the chloroplast would keep doing or stop making - then check the lights toggle on the model.

**Practice item:** On the model, point to the direction light enters and then name the two things a chloroplast makes (food and oxygen).

## 5. See where it belongs

The chloroplast lives inside a green leaf cell of the plant. Packed leaf cells with lots of chloroplasts give the leaf its green color and make it the plant's kitchen. It works with stomata to bring CO2 and with xylem to bring water, so the whole leaf becomes a little photosynthesis machine.

## 6. Compare it

Compare a chloroplast to a **mitochondrion**. The chloroplast captures sunlight to build sugar food - only plants do this. The mitochondrion releases the energy out of food - both plants and animals do that. One is a food maker, the other is a food burner. They pair together to feed a cell.

## 7. Use it in a task

A leaf sits under a heavy shade and gets little light. Predict what happens to the photosynthesis happening in its many chloroplasts if the light is greatly reduced - and give the reason at what is missing. Then say what a plant's many chloroplasts provide for all life.

**Checkpoint:** can you name what the chloroplast needs and the two things it makes, and name the green pigment inside it?
