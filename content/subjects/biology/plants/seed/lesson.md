# Seed

*Biology > Plants > Plant Reproduction > Plant organs > Seed*

A seed is a baby plant in a box. It contains a tiny embryo (the baby plant), a tough seed coat that protects it, and stored food the young plant can use to get started.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a seed cut open to reveal the inside. The tough outer seed coat is on the outside, the stored food area fills most of the seed, with the small embryo tucked to one side. A small sprout is shown nearby.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate the seed and zoom in. Toggle the layers to peel away the seed coat, then reveal the embryo sitting in the stored food. Slide the germination control to watch the seed take in water and the tiny root and shoot begin to push-out.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-seed-coat` to label the protective outer layer, `hs-embryo` to label the baby plant inside, and `hs-food` to label the stored food. Tap `hs-sprout` to label the new shoot and root that appear during germination.

## 4. Explain what it does

A seed waits for the right conditions. Follow what happens: the seed lands and, when it gets water, warmth, and air → the seed coat soften and the seed takes in water → the embryo wakes up and begins to grow → a tiny root goes down and a shoot grows up → the seedling uses its stored food until leaves can make food. This is germination: the seed growing into a new plant.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you check, predict: does the stored food last forever, or does it only feed the seedling for a short time? Why do leaves become important later?

**Practice item:** Use the model to name the three things inside a seed, and say which one is the actual baby plant.

## 5. See where it belongs

The seed is how a plant is born. It came from a flower's fruit and is the starting point of a whole new plant. When it germinates, it grows into a mature plant that makes roots, stem, leaves, and its own flowers and seeds - so the seed is the connection between one plant generation and the next.

## 6. Compare it

Compare a **seed** to a **spore**. A seed is a tiny package with an embryo and its own stored food into a coat. A spore is much simpler and has no stored food - it is a lighter and simpler way some plants make new ones. Seeds give new plants a head start with food; spores rely on the environment.

## 7. Use it in a task

You plant a seed in a pot and water it, but there is no sunlight. It is placed in a warm room. Predict whether the seed will germinate, and explain the difference between what provides its first food and what it will need from the sun as it grows into a bigger plant.

**Checkpoint:** Can you name the three parts of a seed and describe what it needs to germinate?
