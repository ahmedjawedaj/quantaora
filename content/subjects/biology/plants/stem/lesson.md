# Stem

*Biology > Plants > Plant Structure > Plant organs > Stem*

A stem is the part of a plant that grows upward toward the light. It holds up the leaves and flowers, and it contains two kinds of tubes that move materials around the plant: one carries water up from the roots, and the other carries food down from the leaves.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a single stem rising from a small plant base, with leaves and a flower attached at points along it. A section of the stem is cut open so you can see the inner tubes.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate the model and zoom into the cut-open section. Toggle the two tube views - the water tubes (xylem) and the food tubes (phloem) - to see them as separate colors. Trace the tubes with your eye from the base, up the stem, and into the leaves.

## 3. Name it

Tap the `hs-xylem` hotspot to label the water-carrying tube, the `hs-phloem` hotspot to label the food-carrying tube, and `hs-node` to label where the leaf attaches. Tap `hs-support` to place a marker on the stem that shows its supporting role.

## 4. Explain what it does

The stem does two big jobs: it supports the leaves and flowers so they reach the light, and it transports materials. Walking through a single drop of water: it enters the root, climbs up the stem in a xylem tube, and reaches a leaf to be used to make food. The sugar made there then travels back down the stem in a phloem tube to be used or stored.

**Prediction prompt:** Before toggling, predict: do the water tubes and food tubes carry materials in the same direction, or opposite directions? Now check the model and the cut section to confirm.

**Practice item:** Trace a path on the model: start at the base, follow one water tube up to a leaf, then follow a food tube back down. Name the two tissues you used.

## 5. See where it belongs

The stem is one organ in a connected plant. It links the underground root (which brings water up) to the leaves (which make food) and flowers (which make seeds). Every part of the plant depends on the stem to connect them.

## 6. Compare it

Compare the stem to the **root**. The root grows downward into the soil to anchor the plant and take in water. The stem grows upward toward the light to support leaves and flowers. Even though they share the same tubes, one grows down and absorbs, the other grows up and carries.

## 7. Use it in a task

A plant is strangled in the middle of its stem. Water still flows. Predict what will stop being possible and why - and name the tissue that was damaged. Then describe what the stem must do for the whole plant to live.

**Checkpoint:** Can you explain the two directions of movement in a stem and name which tissue carries water and which carries food?

## 8. Review it later

Come back and open the cut section. Now, from memory, point to which tube carries water up and which carries food down, and state one supporting job of the stem.
