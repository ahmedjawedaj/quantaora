# Root

*Biology > Plants > Plant Structure > Plant organs > Root*

A root is the part of a plant that grows down into the soil. It anchors the plant, soaks up water and minerals, and sometimes stores food. Roots do not photosynthesize - they work underground in the dark.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: an underground root system with the surrounding soil cut away so you can see the whole structure. Notice the thick central core going down, the branching side roots, and the delicate mass of fine root hairs near the tip.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate the model and zoom in along the root toward the tip. Toggle the layers to peel away the outer tissue and reveal the water-conducting tubes in the middle of the root. Zoom close to the tip to see the root cap and the tiny root hairs growing just behind it.

## 3. Name it

Tap the `hs-root-tip` hotspot to label the growing tip. Then tap `hs-root-hairs`, `hs-root-cap`, and `hs-xylem` to label the root hairs, the protective cap, and the inner water tubes. Toggle the storage layer and tap `hs-store` to see where food is held.

## 4. Explain what it does

A root has three main jobs. It anchors the plant in the soil so wind and rain cannot tear it free. It absorbs water and minerals through millions of tiny root hairs - the more surface area, the more water the roots can take in. And it transports that water upward into the stem through xylem tubes in its center.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you look, predict where the root hairs would be most concentrated - near the tip, or spread evenly along the whole root? Then check the model.

**Practice item:** Rotate the root and name the two tissues you see: the outer layer with hairs, and the inner tubes. What does each one do?

## 5. See where it belongs

The root is one part of a whole plant. Water comes in through the root, travels up the stem, and reaches the leaves. So the root works together with the stem, leaves, and the plant's transport tissues. It is the plant organ that connects the plant to the soil.

## 6. Compare it

Compare the root to the **stem**. The root grows downward into the soil to anchor and absorb. The stem grows upward toward the light to support leaves and flowers and to carry water and food. Both contain the same transport tubes, but they grow in opposite directions and do different jobs.

## 7. Use it in a task

You uproot a small plant and plant it in a new pot. Predict what you must do to keep it alive, and explain which part of the plant you are depending on. Then describe what would happen to a plant if its roots could suddenly take in no water at all.

**Checkpoint:** Can you name three jobs of a root and explain how root hairs help it soak up water?
