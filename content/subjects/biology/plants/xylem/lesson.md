# Xylem

*Biology > Plants > Plant Transport > Plant transport tissues > Xylem*

Xylem is the plant's one-way water highway. It carries water and minerals upward from the roots all the way to the leaves.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a tall slice of plant stem cut in half, with the xylem shown as a bundle of long hollow tubes running from the bottom up. Small blue droplets enter at the base (root) and we see them rise through the tubes to the top.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate and zoom in on the tube bundle. Turn the transport layer and watch droplets of water track up a single xylem tube. Toggle the root entry at the base and follow the fluid all the way to a leaf portal at the top.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-tube` to label one xylem tube, `hs-rootentry` to label where the water enters at the root, and `hs-leafend` to label where the water ends in the leaf.

## 4. Explain what it does

Follow a water drop: it is in the soil → it enters the root through the root hairs → it rises into a xylem tube in the root → it travels upward through the stem waiting tissues → it reaches the leaf → the leaf uses it in photosynthesis or lets it out. So the xylem's single job is carrying water and minerals up from the soil to the leaves.

**Prediction prompt:** If a tree is very tall, predict whether the water from the ground can still reach its top leaves, or if it must somehow rise high up the trunk. Then check the leaf portal layer.

**Practice item:** Watch one water drop travel. Name the direction it goes (up or down) and the two end points it links.

## 5. See where it belongs

The xylem runs as part of a plant's tissue system, connecting underground roots to the leaves. It works side by side with phloem - the tube that carries food the other way - and together they are a plant's whole transport system. It also feeds the photosynthesis that leaves carry out.

## 6. Compare it

Compare **xylem** and **phloem**. Xylem carries water and minerals upward from the root. Phloem carries food down from the leaves. One goes only up with water, the other goes mainly down with food.

## 7. Use it in a task

A ring of bark is peeled fully around a tree trunk. Water still rises. Predict what stops happening inside the plant and why, given the two-tube system. Explain what the tubes in the trunk allow.

**Checkpoint:** Can you say which direction xylem water and minerals go, and name the two ends the tube connects?
