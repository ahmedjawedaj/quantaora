# Flower

*Biology > Plants > Plant Reproduction > Plant organs > Flower*

A flower is the part of a plant that makes seeds. It uses colorful petals to attract pollinators, male stamens to make pollen, and a female pistil to receive that pollen so a seed can form.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a single flower cut in half so you can see the inside. On the outside are the petals. Near the center are the slender stamens holding pollen at their tips, and in the very middle is the upright pistil with the seed-making base beneath.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate the flower and zoom in. Toggle the layers to peel back the petals, then isolate the stamens, then the pistil. Switch on the pollination layer and watch small pollen-dust dots travel from the stamen tips to the top of the pistil.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-petal` to label the colored petal, `hs-stamen` to label the male part with pollen, `hs-pistil` to label the female part, and `hs-anther` to label the pollen-making tip of the stamen. Tap `hs-ovary` to show the seed base at the flower's center.

## 4. Explain what it does

Walk through seed-making step by step: a flower's petals attract a bee or bird → the visitor carries pollen made at the stamen's anther → the pollen is delivered to the top of the pistil of another flower → fertilization happens → the flower's base (ovary) grows into a fruit → inside that fruit, seeds form and each can grow into a new plant.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you check, predict: does the pollen have to reach the pistil, or is reaching a petal enough? Predict what must happen for a seed to form.

**Practice item:** Use the model to name the male part from her, then the name female part, and point to where the seed finally forms.

## 5. See where it belongs

The flower is the plant's reproduction organ. It sits on the stem, connected to the whole plant's transport system, which feeds it. After the flower makes seeds, the fruit drops or is carried away so a new plant can grow elsewhere - this is how the plant makes its next generation.

## 6. Compare it

Compare the two parts that work together: the **stamen** and the **pistil**. The stamen is the male part that makes pollen. The pistil is the female part that receives pollen and holds the seed-to-be. They are different halves of one job: making seeds.

## 7. Use it in a task

A farmer removes all the bees from an apple orchard. Predict what will happen to the flowers' ability to make apples, and name which part of the process you think would fail. Then explain what the flower needs for seeds to form.

**Checkpoint:** Can you name the male and female parts of the flower and explain, in order, how a flower turns into a fruit holding seeds?

## 8. Review it later

Come back and reopen the flower. Now, from memory, point to the petal, the stamen, and the pistil, and retell the order of seed-making from pollen to fruit.
