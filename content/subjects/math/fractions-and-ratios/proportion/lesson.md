# Proportion

A proportion is a statement that two ratios are equal — 3:5 = 6:10.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: two recipe cards side by side. The left card feeds 4 people using 2 cups of flour; the right card feeds 8 using 4 cups. Above them, the proportion reads 2/4 = 4/8. Tap `hs-proportion-eq` to label the equality.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate and zoom in. Drag the servings slider to 12 — watch the flour card update to 6 cups while the proportion stays balanced. Then tap on the missing-value puzzle: the model shows 3/5 = x/10 with x blank. Tap the 'solve' control and watch the cross products 3 × 10 and 5 × x light up.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-proportion-eq` to label the equal ratios, `hs-cross-products` to label the cross-multiplication, and `hs-missing-value` to label the unknown x.

## 4. Explain what it does

A proportion keeps two ratios in step. When one scales, the other does too. To solve, use cross products: in a/b = c/d, a × d = b × c. For 3/5 = x/10 that means 3 × 10 = 5 × x, so x = 6.

## 5. See where it belongs

Proportions belong to Fractions And Ratios as the 'equal ratios' sentence. They grow from ratios and equivalent fractions, and connect forward to slope and linear functions in algebra.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you press solve, predict the value of x in 3/5 = x/10. What strategy did you use?

## 6. Compare it

Compare a proportion with a ratio. A ratio is ONE comparison (3:5). A proportion compares two ratios that are equal (3:5 = 6:10). The proportion is what lets you solve for a missing amount.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to solve a scale problem: *A map says 1 cm = 5 km. How many km is 7 cm on the map?* Set up the proportion 1/5 = 7/x, cross multiply 1 × x = 5 × 7, and find x = 35 km.

**Practice item:** Solve x/12 = 2/3 using the cross-product method on the model, and say each step aloud.
