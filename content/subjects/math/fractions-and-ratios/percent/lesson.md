# Percent

Percent means 'out of 100' — 25% is 25 out of every hundred.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a 10-by-10 percent grid with 25 squares lit, and a label reading 25%. Next to it, three cards showing the same amount as a percent, a fraction (25/100), and a decimal (0.25). Tap `hs-grid-percent` to label the grid.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate and zoom in. Drag the fill slider to light 10, 25, 50, then 75 squares — watch the percent label change and the fraction/decimal cards update together. Tap a single square to see that one square equals one percent.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-grid-percent` to label the percent grid, `hs-fraction-card` to label the fraction form, and `hs-decimal-card` to label the decimal form.

## 4. Explain what it does

Percent is a fraction with denominator 100. 25% = 25/100 = 0.25 — three spellings of the same amount. Each square of the grid is one percent. Finding a percent of a number means multiplying: 25% of 80 is 0.25 × 80 = 20.

## 5. See where it belongs

Percent belongs to Fractions And Ratios, alongside fractions and decimals as a third spelling of the same amounts. It links to ratio (part compared to 100) and shows up in every real-world data context.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you drag the slider to 50, predict what fraction and decimal the cards will show.

## 6. Compare it

Compare a percent with a fraction. A fraction can have any denominator — 3/8. A percent always compares to exactly 100 — 37.5%. That fixed scale is why percents make different numbers instantly comparable.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to solve a real problem: *A shirt costs $40 and is 30% off. How much do you save?* Set the grid to 30% and use the percent-of-number rule: 0.30 × 40 = $12.

**Practice item:** On the model, show 25% and write its fraction and decimal forms side by side, then explain how each one names the same amount.

## 8. Review it later

**Checkpoint:** What does 60% mean, and how do you write it as a fraction and as a decimal?
