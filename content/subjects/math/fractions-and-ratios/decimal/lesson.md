# Decimal

A decimal is a fraction written with a decimal point and place value — 0.5 is another name for 1/2.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a 10-by-10 grid of small cubes where 30 of them are lit — and a label reading 0.3 or 3 tenths. Next to it, a place-value strip shows the decimal point with tenths and hundredths columns. Tap `hs-grid` to label the shaded grid.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate and zoom in. Drag the fill slider to shade more cubes — 30, then 50, then 75 — and watch the label change from 0.3 to 0.5 to 0.75. Switch the strip to hundredths mode and watch the same amount appear as 75 hundredths.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-grid` to label the grid, `hs-decimal-point` to label the decimal point, and `hs-place-columns` to label the tenths and hundredths columns.

## 4. Explain what it does

A decimal uses place value after the point to write fractions. 0.3 is 3 tenths; 0.75 is 75 hundredths. The first place is tenths, the second hundredths, the third thousandths. Every decimal equals a fraction — 0.5 = 5/10 = 1/2.

## 5. See where it belongs

Decimals belong to Fractions And Ratios as a sibling way of writing fractions. They connect forward to percents (0.25 = 25%) and build on place value from Number Sense.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you drag the slider to 75, predict what fraction the label will show.

## 6. Compare it

Compare a decimal with a fraction. 3/10 writes three tenths as a ratio; 0.3 writes the same amount using a digit in the tenths place. Different code, same value — pick whichever is easier to use.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to solve a money problem: *You spend $0.25 of a dollar. What decimal and what fraction is that?* Shade 25 cubes and read both forms.

**Practice item:** On the model, show 0.5 and 0.50 side by side. Explain whether they are the same or different amounts and why.

## 8. Review it later

**Checkpoint:** Write 7 tenths as a decimal and as a fraction, and explain which place value comes right after the decimal point.
