# Perimeter

Perimeter is the total distance around a shape — the sum of all its sides.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a rectangular garden bed on a board with fence posts at each corner. A little walker figure circles the bed along the edge, and a running tape shows the distance traveled. Tap `hs-perimeter-outline` to label the perimeter.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate and zoom in. Drag the walker around the edge — the tape total adds up side by side. Press 'auto-walk' and the figure walks the whole loop while the counter climbs. Drag a corner to change the bed's size and press reset to re-walk the new outline. Switch to 'triangle bed' and watch three sides sum instead of four.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-perimeter-outline` to label the perimeter, `hs-side` to label one side, and `hs-walker` to label the path the walker travels.

## 4. Explain what it does

Perimeter is the distance around a shape — every side added together. The rectangle has two lengths and two widths, so walk around and the total is length + width + length + width. It is a single distance, measured in plain units like meters or feet.

## 5. See where it belongs

Perimeter is the outline measurement of polygons, sibling to area (the surface inside). It is built from segment side lengths and applies to triangles, quadrilaterals, and any closed shape. Fences and frames are its real-world home.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you press auto-walk on a 4-by-3 bed, predict the final tape reading.

## 6. Compare it

Compare perimeter with area. The walker measures the outline in units; tiling the bed with squares measures the inside in square units. Two beds can share the same perimeter but hold different amounts of soil inside.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to order fencing: the garden bed is 6 m long and 4 m wide. Walk the perimeter to read the fence length needed (6 + 4 + 6 + 4 = 20 m). Then find how much fence a 3-by-3 square bed needs.

**Practice item:** On the model, find the perimeter of a triangle bed with sides 3, 4, 5, then state the walk's total.
