# Quadrilateral

A quadrilateral is any closed shape with four straight sides and four angles adding to 360°.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a flat square plate on a board, with its four sides, four corner dots, and a rotating ring of family cards around it — square, rectangle, rhombus, parallelogram, trapezoid. Tap `hs-quad-plate` to label the quadrilateral.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate and zoom in. Drag a top corner — the plate flexes like a gate, but the four angle tags always add to 360°. Tap each family card to morph the plate: press 'square', then 'rhombus', and watch the same four equal sides lean into a diamond. Press 'brace it' and a diagonal segment snaps in — the plate stiffens, splitting into two triangles.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-quad-plate` to label the quadrilateral, `hs-side` to label a side, and `hs-corner` to label a corner vertex.

## 4. Explain what it does

A quadrilateral has four straight sides, four vertices, and four angles that always sum to 360°. Without a diagonal it flexes freely — push it and a square becomes a rhombus. Add the diagonal and it locks, because it becomes two rigid triangles.

## 5. See where it belongs

Quadrilaterals are four-sided members of the polygon family. The 360° angle sum comes straight from splitting into two triangles (180° each). They connect to perimeter (add four sides) and area (length × width for rectangles).

**Prediction prompt:** Before you press 'rhombus', predict what will stay the same about the four sides.

## 6. Compare it

Compare a square with a rhombus: both have four equal sides, but the square's corners are all right angles while the rhombus leans. Compare a quadrilateral with a triangle: four sides sum to 360°, three sides sum to 180°, and the triangle is rigid while the quadrilateral flexes.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to brace a garden gate: start with the plain square frame and push — it sways. Add the diagonal brace ('brace it') and push again — it holds. Explain why the gate designer added that single wooden diagonal.

**Practice item:** On the model, morph the plate into a rectangle and read its four angles, then sum them aloud.

## 8. Review it later

**Checkpoint:** What is a quadrilateral, why do its angles add to 360°, and how does a diagonal stop it from flexing?
