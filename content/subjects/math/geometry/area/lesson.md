# Area

Area is the surface a shape covers, counted in square units.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a flat rectangle divided into a grid of unit squares. Each square is outlined, and a counter reads the total. A small 4-by-3 rectangle sits on top with '4 × 3 = 12' on a plate. Tap `hs-area-grid` to label the area.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate and zoom in. Drag the rectangle's corner to change its width and height — the square tiles re-layout and the counter updates live. Press 'count' and each unit square flashes as the total counts up. Press 'triangle half' and the rectangle splits diagonally, showing the triangle taking exactly half the squares.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-area-grid` to label the area, `hs-unit-square` to label one square unit, and `hs-rectangle-side` to label the length that multiplies the width.

## 4. Explain what it does

Area counts how many unit squares fill the shape. A rectangle of length 4 and width 3 holds 4 × 3 = 12 unit squares, so its area is 12 square units. The formula length × width is just fast counting. A triangle is exactly half a rectangle, so its area is ½ × base × height.

## 5. See where it belongs

Area belongs to measuring shapes in geometry. It builds on perimeter's parent rectangle and connects to triangle and circle area formulas. In real life it measures floors, fields, and paint coverage.

**Prediction prompt:** Before you drag the rectangle to 5 by 4, predict how many unit squares the counter will show.

## 6. Compare it

Compare area with perimeter. The perimeter is the length of the outline — walk around the shape. The area is the surface inside — cover the shape with squares. A long thin rectangle can have a big perimeter but a small area.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to tile a floor: set the room rectangle to 6 units wide and 4 units long. Count the unit squares to find the floor area (6 × 4 = 24 squares). Then find how many tiles you need if each tile covers one square unit.

**Practice item:** On the model, make a 5-by-3 rectangle and state its area, then halve it diagonally and state the triangle's area.

## 8. Review it later

**Checkpoint:** What does area measure, why is it in square units, and how do you find a rectangle's area?
