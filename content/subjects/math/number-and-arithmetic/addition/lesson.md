# Addition

Addition is putting two or more groups together to find a total.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a base-ten block array on a flat mat — 3 separate cubes on the left, 4 separate cubes on the right, and a labeled number line across the front. Tap the `hs-addends` hotspot to label both groups and the `hs-sum` hotspot to label the combined total.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate the scene and zoom in. Drag the two groups of cubes together and watch them merge into a single group of 7. Then switch the number line into count-on mode: a marker starts at 3 and you tap it forward one step at a time to reach 7.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-addends` to label the addends, `hs-sum` to label the sum, and `hs-count-on` to label the count-on arrow that walks the number line from 3 to 7.

## 4. Explain what it does

Addition combines groups to answer 'how many in all?'. Addend + addend = sum. It is commutative (3 + 4 = 4 + 3) and it is the inverse of subtraction — subtracting the other addend gets you back to the starting number.

## 5. See where it belongs

Addition belongs to Arithmetic, the branch that changes numbers through operations. It builds directly on place value (for regrouping) and the number line (for jumps), and it feeds multiplication, which is repeated addition of equal groups.

**Practice item:** On the model, build 5 + 3 by dragging the two cube groups together, then say the sum aloud.

## 6. Compare it

Compare addition with multiplication. Addition combines any groups — equal or not. Multiplication is repeated addition of equal groups only: 3 × 4 = 4 + 4 + 4. Same idea, different meaning.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to check a real addition with regrouping: *27 + 38*. In the column view, add the ones column (7 + 8 = 15) and explain where the extra ten goes before adding the tens.

**Practice item:** The model shows 14 + 9. Predict the sum first, then drag the cubes to verify — does regrouping change the answer?

## 8. Review it later

**Checkpoint:** Using the model's language (addends, sum, regroup), explain how to add 46 + 27 and why the ones column's total of 13 requires carrying.
