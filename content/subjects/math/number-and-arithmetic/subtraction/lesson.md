# Subtraction

Subtraction takes away from a group or compares two amounts to find a difference.

## 1. See it

Look at the 3D model: a mat with 8 cubes in a line and a gap where 3 cubes can be removed, plus a number line along the front. Tap `hs-minuend` to label the starting group of 8, `hs-difference` to label the 5 that remain, and watch the `hs-subtrahend` hotspot mark the 3 cubes that get taken away.

## 2. Touch it

Rotate and zoom in. Drag 3 cubes out of the group and into a removal tray — watch the count drop from 8 to 5. Then switch to compare view: line up the 8 cubes with a separate row of 5 cubes and highlight the 3 that are extra.

## 3. Name it

Tap `hs-minuend`, `hs-subtrahend`, and `hs-difference` to label the three parts. Tap `hs-left-jump` to label the leftward jump that models subtraction on the number line.

## 4. Explain what it does

Subtraction finds what remains after taking away, or the difference between two amounts. 8 − 3 = 5: the minuend (8), minus the subtrahend (3), equals the difference (5). It is the inverse of addition, so 5 + 3 = 8 and 8 − 3 = 5 belong to the same fact family.

## 5. See where it belongs

Subtraction is one of the four arithmetic operations. It builds on the number line (jumping left) and place value (borrowing), and it works hand-in-hand with addition as its opposite operation.

**Practice item:** On the model, start with 9 cubes, drag 4 into the removal tray, and say the subtraction fact aloud with its difference.

## 6. Compare it

Compare subtraction with addition. Addition combines groups; subtraction takes them apart. The same three numbers make both facts — 6 + 4 = 10 gives 10 − 4 = 6.

## 7. Use it in a task

Use the model to solve a compare problem: *You have 14 stickers and your friend has 9. How many more do you have?* Use the compare view to line up 14 against 9 and read the difference.

**Practice item:** In column view, subtract 52 − 27. Explain what you borrow from the tens column to subtract 7 from 2.

## 8. Review it later

**Checkpoint:** Explain what borrowing means when you subtract 62 − 35, and say which of the pair 7 + 6 = 13 or 13 − 6 = 7 is the subtraction fact.
